# on-screen-keyboard-deactivator-accessibility-service-app
An Android accessibility service app for disabling/enabling on-screen keyboards in one-click. For Android users who want to use an external or physical keyboard instead on their Android device. 


**How to Use:**
1. Open the app and click on the button or go to your Android phone's accessibility settings.
2. Find "On-Screen Keyboard Deactivator" under downloaded services and enable it.
3. The shortcut accessibility button will appear. You can click on it to disable or enable your phone's on-screen keyboard.
